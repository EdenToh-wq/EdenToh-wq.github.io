About Me

My name is Eden Toh Wan Qian. I'm currently a graphic design student at George Brown College - School of Design in Toronto. Through my studies, I have developed a strong interest in visual communication, typography, and layout-driven design across both print and digital media.


My design journey began through hands-on projects that challenged me to think beyond aesthetics and focus on clarity, structure, and user experience. I enjoy pushing myself outside of my comfort zone by experimenting with new ideas and approaches, while continuously improving my technical and conceptual skills.


Outside of design, I enjoy illustration as a creative outlet, which allows me to explore visual expression in a more personal and experimental way. Get to know more about me through social media.

Thoughts on Design and Process

To me, design functions as a visual language that allows people to understand information before any words are read. In many cases, our first impression of a subject is formed through visual experience, making design a crucial tool for communication.

In today's fast-paced society, design plays an important role in helping information be recognized and remembered quickly. Effective design must establish clarity and identity within a short amount of time, guiding the viewer's attention rather than overwhelming it.

For this reason, I believe design is not simply about appearing minimal or aesthetically pleasing, but about creating a strong and meaningful visual impression that communicates ideas clearly and effectively.
